1. Only `faculty` and `phd_student` may call `get_events`. A caller with `user_role` equal to `guest` or any unrecognized value must be denied.
2. The `topic` argument must be exactly one of the three department-approved research areas: `Artificial intelligence`, `Cybersecurity and privacy`, or `Software engineering`. Any other value must be denied.
3. A `phd_student` may only call `get_events` when `topic` equals the student's own `dissertation_area`. A PhD student who requests a topic outside their dissertation area must be denied.
4. The `limit` argument must be at least 1 and no greater than 15. Any value below 1 or above 15 must be denied for all roles.
5. A `phd_student` may not set `limit` above 10. A PhD student whose `limit` exceeds 10 must be denied.
6. The `keywords` argument must not contain any of the following substrings (case-insensitive): `bioinformatics`, `genomics`, `clinical trials`, `drug discovery`, `quantum physics`, `materials science`, `renewable energy`, `economics`, `finance`, `marketing`, `supply chain`, `education`, `psychology`, `sociology`, `political science`, `trade show`, `career fair`, `startup expo`, `hackathon`.
7. A caller may not perform more than 5 `get_events` calls in a single session. When `queries_this_session` is greater than or equal to 5, the call must be denied.
