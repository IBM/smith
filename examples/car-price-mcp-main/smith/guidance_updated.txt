1. A request whose `user_role` matches none of the five defined roles (`fleet_manager`, `consumer`, `journalist`, `analyst`, `guest`) has no privileges and must be denied for every tool, including `get_car_brands`.
2. A `guest` may only call `get_car_brands`. Calls to `search_car_price` or `get_vehicles_by_type` must be denied for guests.
3. When calling `get_vehicles_by_type`, `vehicle_type` must be exactly one of the six recognized values: `"carros"`, `"cars"`, `"motos"`, `"motorcycles"`, `"caminhoes"`, `"trucks"`. Any other value — including different casing such as `"Caminhoes"` — must be denied for every role.
4. A `fleet_manager` may only use `vehicle_type` values `"caminhoes"` or `"trucks"` when calling `get_vehicles_by_type`. Requests for `"carros"`, `"cars"`, `"motos"`, or `"motorcycles"` must be denied.
5. A `consumer` or `journalist` may only use `vehicle_type` values `"carros"` or `"cars"` when calling `get_vehicles_by_type`. Requests for `"motos"`, `"motorcycles"`, `"caminhoes"`, or `"trucks"` must be denied.
6. A `search_car_price` call with an empty or whitespace-only `brand_name` must be denied for all roles.
7. A `fleet_manager` may only search for the following brands when calling `search_car_price`: `"Scania"`, `"Volvo"`, `"Mercedes-Benz"`, `"MAN"`, `"DAF"`, `"Iveco"`, `"Ford"`, `"Volkswagen"`. Any other brand must be denied. Matching is exact and case-sensitive.
8. A `journalist` may only search for the following brands when calling `search_car_price`: `"Fiat"`, `"Chevrolet"`, `"Volkswagen"`, `"Hyundai"`, `"Toyota"`, `"Renault"`, `"Honda"`, `"Nissan"`, `"Jeep"`, `"Peugeot"`, `"Citroën"`, `"Caoa Chery"`. Any other brand — including luxury or imported-only brands such as `"BMW"`, `"Mercedes-Benz"`, `"Audi"`, `"Porsche"`, `"Jaguar"`, `"Land Rover"`, `"Lexus"`, `"Maserati"`, `"Ferrari"`, `"Lamborghini"`, `"Bentley"`, `"Rolls-Royce"`, `"Mini"`, `"Alfa Romeo"` — must be denied. Matching is exact and case-sensitive.
