1. All employees can only access internal repositories.
2. All employees cannot access other teams' repositories.
3. Only HR employees can access compensation records, including salary figures, compensation bands, and salary adjustments.
4. Only employees with the view_ssn permission may request SSN data in compensation lookups; any caller without this permission must not set include_ssn to true.
5. Only engineers and security team members may search repositories; all other roles are blocked from the repository search tool.
6. HR employees may adjust compensation without additional approval for amounts up to $10,000; any compensation adjustment greater than $10,000 requires prior manager approval indicated by the has_approval flag set to true.
7. Emails must not contain Social Security Numbers in their subject line or body; any attempt to send an email with an SSN pattern in the subject or body must be blocked.
8. Compensation adjustment amounts must not be negative; any attempt to adjust compensation by a negative amount must be blocked.
